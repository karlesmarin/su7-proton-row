  ANCHOR_SECTION_31.md s6 names three candidates for the missing structure -- a
  cosine argument outside {1,2,3} pi n a, an n-dependence that is not 1/n^5, or
  multiplets not in (69),(70).  It never names a fourth, and our own
  GATE_POLYAKOV_TWOLOOP.md already contains it:

  (a) PURE GAUGE, two loops.  Dumitru-Guo-Korthals Altes, PRD 89 (2014) 016009,
      eq. (66):   Omega(2)/Omega(1) = -5 g^2 C2(A) / (16 pi^2),
      'independent of the eigenvalues of the loop ... for any of the classical
      and exceptional groups'.  A MULTIPLICATIVE, background-independent
      rescaling -- which s4 and s6 have already excluded, because the lambda
      the a_min column wants is not one number.

  (b) WITH FERMIONS.  Guo-Du, JHEP 05 (2019) 042: 'the surprisingly simple
      proportionality ... is in general NO LONGER TRUE when fermions are taken
      into account', and their expressions carry Bernoulli polynomials of the
      individual background angles AND OF THEIR PAIRWISE DIFFERENCES.
      Our angles are the Wilson-line charges q.  A product B(C_b) B(C_d)
      generates cosines at c_b +- c_d -- and with c in {1,2,3} that reaches
      c in {0,...,6}.  THREE OF THOSE, c = 4, 5, 6, ARE OUTSIDE {1,2,3}.
      That is s6's first candidate, arrived at from the physics rather than
      from a scan.
