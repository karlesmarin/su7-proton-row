VERDICT
============================================================================================
   At the g4 = 0.63 this series uses, the transplant gives w -> 1.03976 against a ceiling of
   1.03846: OVER, by 0.13%. The crossing sits at g4* = 0.6205, which is 1.5% below 0.63.

   The direction is fixed by group theory alone and does not depend on the coupling: since
   6C_F < 5N for every N >= 2, the gauge sector is suppressed the more of the two, so w RISES.
   Only whether it rises far enough depends on g4, and at the nominal value it does.

   This is a transplant of four-dimensional thermal results into a six-dimensional orbifold.
   The group factors are the same algebra; the loop integrals are not. It does not settle the
   open question. What it settles is that the question cannot be assumed to fall the safe way:
   the closest published analogue falls the other way, at the nominal coupling, by less than
   half a percent -- which is inside any honest uncertainty on g4 and on the transplant itself.
