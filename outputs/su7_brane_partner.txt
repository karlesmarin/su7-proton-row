B -- WHAT THE 48 ACTUALLY CARRIES, COUNTED HONESTLY
==============================================================================
  A lepton doublet, all-left-handed, is an SU(3) singlet SU(2) doublet of
  Y = -1/2. Y = +1/2 is its CONJUGATE, which is the same physical state in a
  real representation and not a second generation.

  (7,4bar)  Y = -1/2  extra = -1/2  rung = 2    lepton doublet
  (7,5bar)  Y = -1/2  extra = -1/2  rung = 2    lepton doublet
  (4,7bar)  Y = 1/2   extra = 1/2   rung = 0    its conjugate
  (5,7bar)  Y = 1/2   extra = 1/2   rung = 0    its conjugate

  >> 2 states of Y = -1/2 and 2 conjugates. So the 48 carries ONE lepton
     doublet -- an SU(2) pair -- at rung 2. NOT four doublets at two rungs,
     which is how su7_can_the_adjoint_host.py reported it and how the paper
     now repeats it: the rung-0 pair IS the rung-2 pair conjugated, and a
     conjugate is not a second generation.

C2 -- THE LAST THING THAT COULD KILL THIS: DOES THE DOUBLET EVEN SURVIVE?
==============================================================================
  A component of a bulk multiplet gives a 4D zero mode only if its orbifold
  parity matches. su7_anomaly_channels.py's rule, from their eqs. (37)-(40):
  a weight survives in a multiplet of parities (eta, eta') iff
  P5 P5' of that component equals eta*eta'. If the 48's doublet fails this in
  a 48(+,+), there is no generation to host and the whole question dies here.

  (7,4bar)  P5P5' = +1   survives in a 48(+,+) (eta*eta' = +1): YES
  (7,5bar)  P5P5' = +1   survives in a 48(+,+) (eta*eta' = +1): YES

  >> 2 of 2 survive. The doublet is NOT projected out.
     (Control that had to be able to fire: of the 48's ten states at
     |q| = 1/2, only two carry P5P5' = +1 -- su7_gauge_from_group.py's
     table -- so this test rejects 8 of 10 and is not vacuous.)

E -- THE CONSEQUENCE, STATED BEFORE IT IS ARGUED AWAY
==============================================================================
  rows surviving the vacuum condition after donating an 84 : (1) (2) (4) (5)
  rows that COULD instead donate a 48, and survive at no cost: (3) (4)

  The nu_R condition admits only rows (2) and (3) (sect. 3). Intersecting:
    with the 84 route  : (2)   <- the paper's headline
    with the 48 route  : (3)

  Case (3) is the row with three 48s. If the 48 route is open it pays
  nothing, keeps 9/8, and survives -- and the headline is false.

  What is NOT an obstruction, checked rather than assumed:
   - anomalies. The assignment that needs rung 2 is l = (1/2, 1/2, -1/2) at
     X_Q = -1/18, and it is one of the FOURTEEN that cancel all six channels
     (su7_family_u1.py). It was rejected by sect. 4 on rungs alone.
   - the charge lattice. extra = -1 is on the bulk lattice (1/2)Z anyway, so
     not even the permissive reading is needed.
   - the doublet. It is there, in the 48, with the right Y and the right
     extra, as section B shows.

  So the exclusion of rung 2 does NOT rest on anomalies, charges or content.
  It rests on the partner having to be reachable by <A_5>, i.e. on the Yukawa
  being GAUGE-GENERATED. A brane partner is not reachable by <A_5>: its
  Yukawa would be a brane-localised coupling with a free coefficient. That is
  a real price -- it is the calculability that gauge-Higgs unification exists
  to buy, and Maru's own review calls this obstruction generic -- but it is a
  price, not a prohibition, and this paper cannot claim otherwise.
