C -- AND THEIR OWN DECOMPOSITION LISTS ALREADY SAID SO
==============================================================================
  Their sect. 3.2 lists each multiplet's SU(2)_L content. An SU(2) multiplet
  of dimension d has members q = (d-1)/2, ..., -(d-1)/2, so it contributes a
  charged pair at EVERY c = d-1, d-3, ... down to 1 or 0 -- a quadruplet at
  c = 3 and again at c = 1. Fold their lists that way and compare to B.

  rep   their SU(2) list           folded to c        from the group    
  7     {2: 1}                     {1: 1}             {1: 1}             
  28    {3: 1, 2: 5}               {2: 1, 1: 5}       {2: 1, 1: 5}       
  48    {3: 1, 2: 10}              {2: 1, 1: 10}      {2: 1, 1: 10}      
  84    {4: 1, 3: 5, 2: 15}        {3: 1, 2: 5, 1: 16} {3: 1, 2: 5, 1: 16} 

  their published lists and SU(7) agree on all four multiplets: True

  >> So the extra term is not a repair of anything of theirs. Their eq. (76)
     and their sect. 3.2 list are consistent with each other and with the
     group; what is naive is reading c = 1 as 'a doublet'. The 84 has 15
     doublets AND a quadruplet, and the quadruplet's inner pair is the
     sixteenth term at c = 1. Their note under eq. (71) says exactly this.

  Consequence for our own control: su7_vacuum's CONTROL 1 maps c -> SU(2)
  dimension as d = c+1 and needs r4 OFF to pass. That mapping is only valid
  when no multiplet reaches past its top member, which the 84's quadruplet
  does. The control's CONCLUSION survives -- the last two terms of eq. (76)
  must be 84 terms, since as 48 terms the doublet count cannot be made to
  work either way -- but it passes for a reason narrower than it claims, and
  section C above is the version that does not depend on the c+1 shortcut.

D -- WHAT THIS EXCLUDES
==============================================================================
  the 48 is the adjoint, and of its 48 generators only 22 carry Wilson-line
  charge -- the other 26 are invisible to V_eff at any order in alpha.

  Their eq. (76)'s 48 block counts exactly those 22, split 2 and 4 and 16 by P5P5'.
  So the term list for the 48 is right, and it is right for the same reason
  and by the same rule as the 7, the 28 and the 84.

  >> The two rows of their Table 1 we fail on are the two containing a 48.
     After this, that failure cannot be a miscount of the 48: the block is
     derived from SU(7) itself. Whatever differs between their alpha_min and
     ours is downstream of the term lists, not inside them.

  And one thing this changed about OUR OWN reading, not theirs. su7_vacuum's
  r4 flag is justified in its docstring as a second, smaller mass EIGENVALUE
  for the 84's SU(2) quadruplet -- a second eigenvalue of the same states.
  Section B shows that is not what it is: it is the quadruplet's twelfth
  STATE, a_+a_+a_-, which a state count does check and did. The flag was
  right; the reason we gave for it was not.
