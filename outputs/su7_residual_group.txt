==============================================================================
  bulk charge lattice: 1/2 Z, quantum 1/2 (section 6)

  q_phi      from     residual     meaning
  1/2        7        Z_1          Z_1 -- NOTHING survives: the VEV charge IS the quantum
  1          28       Z_2          Z_2
  3/2        84       Z_3          Z_3

  >> q_phi = 3/2, the (777) of the 84, leaves Z_3.  That is the group the
     mod-3 argument of section 6 is about, and the paper never names it.

B -- WHAT SURVIVES Z_N, BY TWO ROUTES
==============================================================================
  Route 1 (the paper): an operator of charge A can be dressed iff A/q_phi is
  an integer.  Route 2 (the group): A must be trivial under the residual Z_N,
  i.e. A/QUANTUM = 0 mod N.  If the two disagree anywhere, one is wrong.

  q_phi    A         route 1     route 2     agree?
  1/2      -1        dressable   dressable   yes
  1/2      1/2       dressable   dressable   yes
  1/2      2         dressable   dressable   yes
  1/2      7/2       dressable   dressable   yes

  1        -1        dressable   dressable   yes
  1        1/2       forbidden   forbidden   yes
  1        2         dressable   dressable   yes
  1        7/2       forbidden   forbidden   yes

  3/2      -1        forbidden   forbidden   yes
  3/2      1/2       forbidden   forbidden   yes
  3/2      2         forbidden   forbidden   yes
  3/2      7/2       forbidden   forbidden   yes

  disagreements between the two routes, over all m in [-4,4] and all q_phi: 0
  >> the selection rule IS the residual group, and not a separate assumption.

==============================================================================
C -- WHAT A SECOND VACUUM EXPECTATION VALUE DESTROYS
==============================================================================
  With two breaking scalars the residual group is generated by gcd(q1,q2),
  so the protection is only as strong as the SMALLEST charge that condenses.
  The paper states this and moves on. Here is the whole table, which is what
  a scalar sector has to be built against.

  pair               gcd        residual     protection at GS charges
  1/2 + 1            1/2        Z_1          DESTROYED
  1/2 + 3/2          1/2        Z_1          DESTROYED
  1 + 3/2            1/2        Z_1          DESTROYED

  single scalars, for comparison:
  1/2 alone          1/2        Z_1          DESTROYED
  1 alone            1          Z_2          DESTROYED
  3/2 alone          3/2        Z_3          holds

  >> only q_phi = 3/2 ALONE protects.  Add the 7's scalar and gcd(3/2,1/2)
     = 1/2 collapses the residual to Z_1; add the 28's and gcd(3/2,1) = 1/2
     does the same.  So the scalar sector Part VII builds must give a VEV to
     the 84's singlet and to NEITHER of the other two -- that is a condition
     on the scalar potential, and it is the first thing that potential has to
     be shown to do.

  Note what this does NOT settle: why the potential prefers that direction.
  Naming the group is group theory; making it dynamical is a potential, and
  that is the next piece, not this one.
