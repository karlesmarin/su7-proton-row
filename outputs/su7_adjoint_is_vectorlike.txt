A -- IS THE SURVIVING 4D SPECTRUM OF A 48 CHIRAL?
==============================================================================
  For every surviving component, look for a survivor with EXACTLY opposite
  Y and extra at the SAME 4D chirality. That pair can be given a gauge-
  invariant Dirac mass, so neither member is chiral matter.

  48(+1,+1):  18 survivors,  18 of them have a same-chirality conjugate
               UNPAIRED (i.e. chiral): 0
  48(+1,-1):  24 survivors,  24 of them have a same-chirality conjugate
               UNPAIRED (i.e. chiral): 0

  And the reason it can never be otherwise, which is one line:
  the conjugate of (i,jbar) is (j,ibar), and
      P5P5'(j,ibar) = p_j p_i = P5P5'(i,jbar)   -> survives with it
      P5  (j,ibar) = P5_j P5_i = P5  (i,jbar)   -> at the same chirality
  It is not a property of this Wilson line or of these parities. It is that
  the adjoint is its own conjugate.
  verified on all 42 components: True

==============================================================================
  If Sym^2 and Sym^3 were vector-like too, this test would be measuring the

==============================================================================
C -- WHAT THIS DOES TO THE BRANE QUESTION
==============================================================================
  su7_brane_partner.py asked whether a brane-localised charged-lepton partner
  could complete the 48's rung-2 doublet. The answer is that there is nothing
  to complete. The doublet comes with its own conjugate, at the same 4D
  chirality, and the pair is massive. A brane field would be the partner; the
  defect is one step earlier, in the doublet itself.

  So the exclusion of the 48 in sect. 4 does NOT rest on the Yukawa being
  gauge-generated, and the headline does not need that precondition. It rests
  on the 48 being a real representation, which no model-building choice can
  change and no localised field can repair.

  It also explains their own sentence, which we had taken as a stipulation:
  the 7, 28, 48 and 84 are introduced with the Higgs potential as their only
  role. For the 48 that is not a choice -- it is the only thing a real
  representation CAN do here.

  And it generalises past this paper: on this orbifold no real representation
  can furnish a chiral generation, so the rung ladder of sect. 4 never has to
  consider one. The box count and the reality condition between them cover
  every multiplet their model contains.
